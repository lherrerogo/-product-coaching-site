My story

Nobody handed me a product title.

I studied advertising in Madrid. I didn't have a computer science degree, a Silicon Valley network or anyone to tell me “product manager” was even a job.

What I had was curiosity, a suitcase and a habit of saying yes before I felt ready. Ten years later I've launched products in five countries, for a global bank, the world's biggest beverage company and Europe's connected roads.

Here's how that happened, and what each step taught me.

FAQ

Good questions.

Why work with me when I can just ask AI?

Honestly, you should use AI. I use it every day and I'll show you how to use it well in product work. But AI gives you a good average answer to the question you asked. I help you find the question you should be asking.

I know your context because I've listened to it. I've made the mistakes, sat in the interviews and been in the meetings. I'll notice when you're underselling yourself, hold you accountable week after week, and open doors through my network. AI can't do any of that for you.

I don't have a tech background. Can I still become a PM?

Yes. I didn't have one either. Many great PMs come from marketing, sales, operations, design or consulting. We'll work out which skills you already have, what's genuinely missing, and the most realistic route in for you.

How is coaching different from a course or bootcamp?

A course teaches everyone the same curriculum. Coaching starts from you: your background, your target role and your blockers. It's also a fraction of the price of most product masters.

How do the hour packages work?

You buy a bundle of hours and use them at your pace, typically one session a week or every two weeks, within the validity period. Larger bundles bring the hourly rate down from €120 to €92.50. You can pay the bigger packages in instalments.

Can my company pay for it?

Often, yes. Many employers have a learning & development budget. I can provide an invoice and a short proposal for your manager.

Where and in which language?

Mostly online by video, or in person in Amsterdam or Marbella/Málaga. Sessions in English or Spanish, and Dutch if you're patient with me.

What happens on the intro call?

20 minutes, no pitch. You tell me what's on your mind, I ask a few questions, and we both decide whether working together makes sense. If it doesn't, I'll point you to someone or something better.
